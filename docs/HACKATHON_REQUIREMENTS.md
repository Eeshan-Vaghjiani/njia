## Submission essentials

- **Deadline:** Sunday 27 September 2026, **19:30 EAT / 17:30 Tunis / 16:30 UTC**.
- Submit **once through the official form**, using the final confirmed team name, lead details, country and attendance mode. Save the confirmation receipt. Publishing code/assets does not submit an entry.
- Required: a **working prototype or interactive demo**, project/team details, award choices, AI/tool disclosure and final confirmation.
- The form requires **three URLs: source code, presentation and a 90-second demo video**. It accepts accessible links, not file uploads; a hosted PDF is a valid presentation.
- **Public deployment, Docker and NVIDIA Brev are optional.** A local app demonstrated in the video with run instructions is accepted. A live URL does not replace the three required URLs.
- The published requirements do **not explicitly require voice narration**, a slide template or a slide count. A working, clear 90-second demo is required; [VOICEOVER.md](VOICEOVER.md) is a ready supporting script.
- Teams may have **1–5 members**. Registration/final team confirmation are closed; existing organizer-confirmed participation governs eligibility. Only the three country winners give scheduled live demos.

### Optional supporting evidence

- **Project cover / screenshot / logo URL — optional**: one accessible JPG/PNG, with permission and no private data; not scored.
- **Live demo URL — optional**: deployed prototype, with brief test steps in README/slides.
- **Testing, results and known limitations — optional**: up to three short bullets with actual results, evidence and an edge case/limitation.
- **Responsible AI and data — optional**: 2–4 sentences covering data source/permission, safeguards and unresolved risks.

The form explicitly states that earlier submissions remain valid without these newly added fields. There is no required separate project-card upload or member-contact list.

For field 10, distinguish implemented features, mocked/unfinished work and hackathon work versus reused components. Field 18 asks for **2–3 sentences per selected award**, including concrete evidence and a video timestamp or repository file. Field 19 separates **AI inside the product** from **AI used to build it**, including an input → AI action → output example, actual tools/assets, constraints and fallback. Keep private contact details and credentials out of public answers.

## Published judging rubric — 100 points

| Exact criterion | Points | Official emphasis |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Problem + user value | 20 | Clear need, credible user, practical value or meaningful delight |
| Functional execution | 20 | Core experience works live, beyond a static concept |
| Quality of AI use | 20 | Quality and relevance of the actual AI contribution; provider-neutral |
| Testing + reliability | 15 | Tests, failure modes, cost, speed and fallbacks |
| Experience + demo | 15 | Understandable, usable product clearly demonstrated in 90 seconds |
| Responsible AI + data | 10 | Privacy, consent, bias, safety, copyright and human oversight |

Brev use or credit spending gives no automatic advantage. All eligible entries receive automatic country-podium consideration; primary prize preference is not an exclusive track or a guarantee of winning.
